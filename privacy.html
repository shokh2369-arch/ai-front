Privacy Policy

start.ai · Effective 23 September 2026 · Last updated 23 September 2026

In short
  • We use what you tell the assistant (your learning goal and answers) to build your study plan.
  • Your chat messages are processed by a third-party AI service provider to write the replies.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details.
  • You can delete your data at any time — see Deleting your data.

1. Who we are

start.ai ("the app", "we", "us") is an AI learning coach that turns a learning goal into a scheduled study plan. It is developed and operated by Shohjahon Abdullayev. For any privacy question, contact startmentorai@gmail.com.

This policy covers the start.ai Android app and the start.ai website.

2. Data we collect

Information you give us

  • Display name (optional). If you enter a name in Settings, it is saved on your device and sent to our server so the assistant can greet you by name.
  • Chat messages. What you write to the assistant and the answers you choose: your learning goal, current level, budget, available time, preferred days and deadline, and any questions or change requests about your plan. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your messages.

Information created when you use the app

  • Your plan and progress: phases, tasks, scheduled study sessions, which sessions you mark as done, and the history of changes to your plan.
  • A random user ID and access token that link your device to your plans. They are not tied to your real identity.
  • Time zone and app language, so sessions land on the right days and the assistant replies in your language. The time zone is a region setting such as "Asia/Tashkent"; it is not your location.

Technical data

  • Like any internet service, our hosting providers receive your IP address and basic request details (time, device/browser type) when the app connects. These are used to deliver the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse (for example, daily usage limits).

Stored only on your device

  • Your chat list, study streak, daily-goal minutes and settings (theme, language, sound) are kept in your device's local storage and are not sent to us, except the name and access token described above.

What we do not collect

We do not collect precise or approximate location, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera data, payment information, or advertising IDs. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the assistant and build, schedule and update your study plan.
  • To show your progress and move missed sessions forward.
  • To reply in your language and at the right times for your time zone.
  • To keep the service working, secure and fair to use (rate and daily limits).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. AI processing

To write replies and plans, the content of your chat (your messages and the relevant plan details) is sent to a third-party AI service provider that runs the AI model. The provider processes this data only to return a response to us and is bound by its own privacy and security obligations. AI replies can be wrong; please use your own judgment.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run start.ai, and only as needed:

ProviderPurposeData
AI service providerAI replies and plan generationChat messages and plan details
RenderHosting our server and its databaseAll server-side data listed above, IP address
VercelHosting the websiteIP address and request details

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

Shop and course links. The Kit section may show "Search in" links to Uzum Market, Yandex Market, Coursera, Udemy or Stepik. They open that site's own search for an item; only the item name is included in the link, never your personal data. Once you're on their site, their privacy policy applies.

6. How long we keep data

  • Server data (name, chats, plans, progress, user ID) is kept while you use start.ai, and deleted when you ask us to delete it.
  • Server and hosting logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.
  • Data on your device stays until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your device: open Settings → Data → Reset app data (or "Clear all chats"), or uninstall the app.
  • On our server: email startmentorai@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my start.ai data" and include your User ID from Settings → Data. We will delete your name, chats, plans and progress within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it, using the same email.

8. Security

All connections between the app and our servers use encryption (HTTPS). Access to your plans requires the random access token stored on your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Children

start.ai is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.

Данные, которые вы сообщаете

Данные, которые появляются при использовании

  • Часовой пояс и язык приложения — чтобы занятия стояли в правильные дни, а ассистент отвечал на вашем языке. Часовой пояс — это настройка региона, например «Asia/Tashkent», а не ваше местоположение.

  • Как и у любого интернет-сервиса, хостинг-провайдеры получают ваш IP-адрес и базовые сведения о запросе (время, тип устройства/браузера). Они нужны для работы сервиса, безопасности и защиты от злоупотреблений (например, дневные лимиты).

Хранится только на устройстве

Мы не собираем точное или приблизительное местоположение, контакты, фото и файлы, данные микрофона и камеры, платёжные данные и рекламные идентификаторы. В приложении нет рекламы и сторонних SDK аналитики или отслеживания.

Поставщик обрабатывает эти данные только для того, чтобы вернуть нам ответ, и соблюдает собственные обязательства по конфиденциальности и безопасности. Ответы ИИ могут быть неточными — полагайтесь и на собственное суждение.
